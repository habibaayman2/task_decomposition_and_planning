# Material Handling Procedures (IronBridge Safety Policy #1)

Applicable department: Warehouse
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose
Defines safe lifting, storage, and transport procedures for construction
materials moving through IronBridge warehouses and out to job sites.

## Key rules the procurement assistant should know
- Materials in the **Steel** and **Concrete** categories require
  mechanical lifting equipment above 50kg per unit — never manual lift.
- **Cement** bags must be stored on pallets, stacked no higher than 10
  bags, away from moisture sources.
- Any material reservation that would drop a warehouse's on-hand
  quantity below its `MinimumStockLevel` triggers the low-stock approval
  workflow (see the Warehouse Safety Regulations policy for the
  reasoning) — this is a supply continuity control, not primarily a
  handling-safety one, but the two are enforced together at the
  warehouse dispatch step.

This document exists so the assistant can read handling context once
per session and reason over it, rather than requiring a purpose-built
tool for every possible material-handling question a site engineer or
warehouse supervisor might ask.
